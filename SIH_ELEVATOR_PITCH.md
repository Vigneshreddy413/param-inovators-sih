# THERMOSENTRY 30-SECOND ELEVATOR PITCH

**What is it?**  
THERMOSENTRY is an AI-assisted satellite thermal intelligence and decision-support platform.

**What problem does it solve?**  
It cures "alert fatigue" for emergency analysts. Raw satellite thermal data flags routine industrial flares exactly the same as catastrophic wildland fires, leading to information overload.

**How does it work?**  
It ingests NASA FIRMS observations and dynamically cross-references them with OpenStreetMap industrial data. It then applies an unsupervised Isolation Forest machine learning model and a Temporal Engine to filter out routine baseline heat, outputting a prioritized list of true, explainable anomalies.

**Why does it matter?**  
THERMOSENTRY doesn't just detect where heat exists—it learns what normal looks like, identifies what changed, explains why it matters, and tells the analyst what needs attention first, drastically optimizing emergency response times.
